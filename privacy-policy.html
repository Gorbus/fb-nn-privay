Privacy Policy

Facebook Page Analytics Dashboard · Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the operator of the Facebook Page Analytics Dashboard (“Dashboard”) processes information when providing private analytics and reporting to a Facebook Page owner and authorized users.

Information processed

The Dashboard may retrieve and process Facebook Page data made available through Meta's Graph API, including Page identifiers, Page Insights metrics, and Page post information such as post text, timestamps, links, and engagement counts. It may also process account identifiers and access information needed to administer and secure dashboard access.

Purpose and use

Data is used to collect, store, analyze, and display Page performance information for reporting and dashboard operation. The operator does not sell Meta Platform Data or use it to build advertising profiles or send unsolicited messages.

Storage and service providers

Dashboard data may be stored in Google Sheets and processed by Google Apps Script and Meta services. These providers process data under their applicable terms and privacy policies. The operator configures access to limit data to authorized people.

Sharing and access

Data is made available only to the Dashboard operator and users authorized by the Page owner/operator. The underlying spreadsheet and access tokens should not be publicly shared or exposed in browser code or URLs.

Retention and deletion

Data is retained in the connected spreadsheet while needed for reporting and until deleted by the operator or following an appropriate request. To request access, correction, or deletion of Dashboard-stored data, contact the operator using the contact details supplied directly by the operator.

Security

The operator uses reasonable safeguards, including access restrictions and confidential handling of API credentials. No internet service or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

Meta

Use of Meta Platform Data is subject to Meta's applicable terms and policies. This Dashboard is independent and is not operated by, sponsored by, or affiliated with Meta Platforms, Inc.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the Dashboard operator using the contact details provided to you directly.

This policy must be reviewed and updated to reflect the actual operator, contact details, data practices, and retention arrangements before production use.
